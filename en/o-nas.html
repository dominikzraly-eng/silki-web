Our story

A family business built on relationships

We build long-term relationships with clients and hairdressers across the Czech Republic.

A personal approach to every piece

We're a family business that cares about quality and about happy customers and partners.

Tereza has worked in the industry for over 10 years. We work long-term with clients and hairdressers across the Czech Republic, and the same rule applies to every piece: we only offer hair we would happily wear ourselves.

We started as a family team and still work the same way today: a fair price based on weight and a personal approach to every order.

Quality you can't fake

We don't treat dark and medium hair with any chemicals, so it keeps its natural structure. That is the difference between hair that lasts and hair that disappoints after a few weeks.

Natural structure

An intact cuticle means natural shine, a smooth fall and a long life.

Hand-selected

We check every piece ourselves. We don't supply anything we wouldn't wear ourselves.

Fair price per gram

The price is based on the weight of the piece. No grey areas.

Passing on the craft

Silki also runs professional keratin hair extension courses. The course takes place in Prague and is taught in Czech.

They are led by experienced trainers Markéta and Tereza, each with years of experience in the industry. They teach the method from the basics and technique through to final styling and a business mindset.
