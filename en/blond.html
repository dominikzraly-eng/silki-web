Blonde

How we make blonde from virgin hair

We show how virgin hair becomes blonde and how we lighten it so the hair keeps as much of its quality as possible.

What is virgin hair?

Virgin hair has never been chemically treated, coloured or bleached.

It has an intact cuticle, full strength and natural shine. That makes it the ideal base for quality blonde. It handles lightening much better than hair that has already been chemically treated.

Lightening with quality in mind

We make blonde from medium and darker virgin hair by bleaching it. The aim is to lighten the hair while disturbing its structure as little as possible. That takes time, experience and a careful process with no shortcuts.

What matters

  • Gradual work. We lighten in stages, never by force in one go.
  • Strength checks. We watch the hair throughout so it keeps its elasticity and shine.
  • Toning. We fine-tune the final shade so the blonde is clean, without unwanted undertones.
  • Care. After lightening, we treat the hair and check it again.
