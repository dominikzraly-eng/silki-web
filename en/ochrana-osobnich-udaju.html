Privacy policy

How we handle your personal data under the GDPR. Effective from 21 November 2026.

This translation is provided for information. The Czech version is legally binding.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is the operator of silkihair.cz. Processing takes place in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR) and Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll., on the Processing of Personal Data.

2. What data we process

We only process data that you give us yourself through the enquiry or registration form or when communicating with us:

  • first name and surname,
  • email address and phone number,
  • for hairdressers and salons, the company name, company ID (IČO) and town or city,
  • the content of your message and the details needed to process an order or course booking (such as billing and delivery details).

3. Purposes and legal bases for processing

  • Handling your enquiry, order or course application and communicating with you: performance of a contract and steps taken before entering into it (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Keeping accounts and meeting legal obligations: compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Replying to a question sent through the form: our legitimate interest, or your consent (Article 6(1)(f) or (a) GDPR).

4. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes above, and then for the period required by law (for example, accounting and tax records for the statutory period). We delete non-binding enquiries that do not lead to an order from our database and our mailbox no later than 12 months after the last contact. Database backups are overwritten and deleted within 30 days at the latest.

5. Recipients and processors

We do not pass your data to third parties for marketing purposes and we do not sell it. Our processors may have access to the data to the extent necessary:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: website hosting and the enquiry database,
  • Web3Forms: delivering submitted forms to our email,
  • Google (Gmail): email inbox,
  • Meta (WhatsApp Ireland Limited): communication via WhatsApp,
  • the carrier when goods are shipped.

Some processors may transfer data to the USA. Such transfers take place under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

6. Cookies

silkihair.cz only uses technically necessary cookies that the website needs to work. We do not use marketing or tracking cookies. If we start using analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

7. Your rights

In relation to your personal data, you have the right in particular:

  • to access your data and have it corrected,
  • to erasure (the ‘right to be forgotten’) and to restriction of processing,
  • to data portability,
  • to object to processing,
  • to withdraw your consent at any time.

You can exercise your rights by emailing tereza.celedova@gmail.com.

8. Supervisory authority

If you believe that the processing of your data has breached data protection rules, you can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, www.uoou.cz.

This privacy policy is effective from 21 November 2026.
