Why work with Silki

Better terms

Registered partners buy at private wholesale prices per gram, leaving room for their own margin.

First pick

You see new pieces before anyone else and can choose the best for your clients.

In-person viewing

You can see and feel the quality for yourself before you decide.

Linked to our course

Combine the partnership with our keratin course and start offering extensions with confidence.

Reliable availability

Regularly restocked hair across lengths and shades, so your salon runs smoothly.

Personal support

We help you choose the right weight and shade. We're here for you after you buy, too.

Four steps to partnership

01

Registration

Fill in the short partner form below and we'll usually get back to you within 24 hours.

02

Consultation

We talk about your salon, volumes and needs, and arrange an in-person viewing and the partner price list.

03

Choosing pieces

You get early access to the range and choose pieces for your clients.

04

Long-term partnership

Regular deliveries, support and the option of training for your team.

Combine the partnership with a course

If you're just adding extensions to your services, we recommend starting with our keratin course. You'll learn the method from A to Z and can go straight on to ordering hair from us. The course takes place in Prague and is taught in Czech.
