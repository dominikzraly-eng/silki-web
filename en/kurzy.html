Hair extension course

A professional course led by experienced Silki Hair trainers. Full theory and practice.

On the course you'll learn the full theory and practice. From preparing classic and micro-keratin bonds to application, sectioning with elastic bands, brushing out, removal and parting. Our priorities are your clients, healthy hair and strands that stay in and don't fall out. The course takes place in Prague and is taught in Czech.

Keratin method extensions

Keratin hair extensions are one of the most popular services in the beauty industry today. On our course, we teach you step by step how to master the technique so you can start building your own client base. When keratin is applied correctly, it is the gentlest method on the market.

What you'll learn on the course

  • Getting to know the tools
  • Preparing hair strands
  • Keratin bonding and re-bonding, from classic bonds to micro keratin
  • Planning the sections correctly
  • Sectioning with elastic bands
  • Applying bonds correctly, in the way that is gentlest on the natural hair
  • Removing strands gently
  • Refitting strands

Not just technique, but mindset too

Our course covers hair extension techniques and a mindset for success. We spend a large part of it on how to think like a successful businesswoman. Success isn't only about what you can do with your hands. It's also about how you think, how much you believe in yourself and whether you can keep going when you're not completely sure.

When you finish the course, you'll know how to do hair extensions and how to build a successful business and a steady client base.
