How to choose and how to care

A practical guide to choosing length and shade, and to everyday care, so you can enjoy your hair for as long as possible.

Choosing the length

We give lengths in centimetres as ranges (for example 51–55 cm). When choosing, always think about where the hair should start and how far it should reach.

  • Measure your current length from the roots down and decide how much you want to add.
  • Shoulder length is usually around 40–50 cm, waist length 60–75 cm.
  • If you can't decide between two lengths, choose the longer one. You can always cut hair shorter, but you can't make it longer.

Choosing the shade

We work with three main categories: dark, medium and blonde. The aim is a smooth blend that nobody notices at first glance.

  • Compare shades in daylight, not under artificial light.
  • Choose by the lengths and ends, not by the regrowth. That's where hair is usually matched.
  • Not sure? Send us a photo of your hair in daylight and we'll advise you.

How many grams do I need?

The weight determines how thick and full the result will be. The price is based on the weight of the piece.

  • Fine hair / a little extra volume: usually a lower weight.
  • Lots of length and fullness: a higher weight, or several pieces.
  • We're happy to help you work out the amount and combine pieces. Just message us.

Everyday care

Virgin hair behaves like your own, and it needs the same care.

  • Use gentle, sulphate-free shampoos and a good conditioner.
  • Brush from the bottom up with a special brush for hair extensions.
  • Always apply heat protection before heat styling.
  • At night, put your hair in a loose plait so it doesn't tangle.

Not sure what to choose?

Message us. We'll help with length, shade and weight, and recommend specific pieces from the shop.
