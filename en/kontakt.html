Frequently asked questions

How is the price calculated?

The price is based on the weight of the piece: total price = weight × price per gram. You'll find the rates per gram by length and shade in our price list.

What does ‘unprocessed’ hair mean?

Hair that keeps its natural structure and has not been chemically treated. It has a natural shine, falls well and lasts longer. Our dark and medium hair is like this, and we lighten blonde gently. We hand-pick every piece.

How do I choose the right length and shade?

Our Care & choosing guide will help. If you're unsure, send us a photo of your hair in daylight and we'll recommend specific pieces.

How do collection and delivery work?

Collection in person is only possible in Prague. We ship across the Czech Republic and to the EU. We'll agree the details after you send your enquiry.

I'm a hairdresser. Do you have wholesale prices?

Yes. We offer salons and hairdressers partner prices, first pick and an in-person viewing. Find out more on our For salons page.

Do you run extension courses?

Yes. We run professional keratin method courses for beginners, hairdressers and whole teams, mainly in Prague and elsewhere in the Czech Republic by arrangement. The course takes place in Prague and is taught in Czech.
